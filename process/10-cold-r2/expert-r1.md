# Harsh expert pass r1 (20 runs, build v2) — condensed

VERDICT: "Good, not very good. A smart Hooke/Gaudí toy with a convincing chain simulation and a real physics payoff", pulled down by toy
stones, a stock gradient sky, a collapse that explodes instead of failing, and grain/jagged edges whenever anything moves.

Real: the chain (alternating links, dull highlights, non-stretch, pendulum slosh); the bulb's soft shadows with link detail; the sacks;
the warm dusty cellar ("could pass for a photo for a second"); the typography; the Gaudí logic (V chain → pointed arch with spire).

The five things, ranked:
1. The collapse lies about masonry: draw the line of thrust (middle third, red near the edge); keep stones rigid while dragging (it
   saw a "rubber arch"); four hinges, three rigid pieces; near-zero bounce; cap push-apart speed.
2. The image dissolves in motion: never below ~0.7× resolution, AA edges, denoise; keep converged static layers; ghost trails.
3. Toy stones in a stock desert: one stone material varying brightness not hue (random tints read as a checkerboard), chipped
   edges, joint shading; a hole at a pointed apex; pinnacles on 45–60° slopes; **sky inconsistent with the sun behind the viewer**
   (should be earth shadow + pink band, front-lit mountains — or face the sun and backlight); salt-crust ridges; shadows softening
   toward tips; a human figure for scale.
4. The loop is slow and shallow: rebuild ≤ 4 s as a time-lapse; input fast-forwards; show a crane or let the user strike the
   centering; move anchors (span), chain length, chains between any two anchors, a hanging net.
5. Sound and world static and synthetic: varied knocks by impact, rope creaks, grinding before failure, bulb hum + cellar reverb,
   gusts; "let the sun set so the bulb becomes the main light and the chain's shadow on the wall becomes the drawing".
Broken: explosive collapse (stones above the crown, one stuck half out of a pillar), ghost trails, centering smears as it
disappears, apex hole, pinnacles intersecting, zoom-out exposes the cellar's side walls, intro timing tied to frame rate.
Sound: clinks plausible; stone set-down = "drum-machine kick plus woodblock" at a mechanical 200–255 ms rhythm; collapse = wash
plus ~25 identical impacts; background one flat low rumble (old build).

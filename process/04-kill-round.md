## What the critics agreed on, across lenses
- **Consequence beats reveal.** Most of the 36 uncover something that already exists (scratch-offs of famous facts). The survivors compute
  something from your gesture that you couldn't predict and hand it back in a form you can check by eye.
- **Famous footage kills surprise.** If the phenomenon has a viral video (singing ice, femto-photography, Néry's freefall), the interactive
  version arrives pre-spent. Prefer phenomena too slow, hidden or internal to film.
- **Muted and phone realities:** sound can't play before the first tap; pull-down drags trigger pull-to-refresh; long-press opens menus;
  the keyboard covers 40% of a phone; the thumb hides whatever is under it.
- **AS HANGS** is the only unanimous keep. Its three ceiling-raisers from different critics converge: the piece performs its own verb once
  before touch; stone never morphs (voussoirs drop into place when the chain settles); the verb works both ways: push up and the stone falls.

## Spike list (3–5)
AS HANGS · STILL WARM · LINOTYPE (the three with ≥3 keeps) + TANGLE (humour; the jaded critic's first pick, so the finalists aren't all art).

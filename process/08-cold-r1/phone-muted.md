# Cold round 1 · phone, muted (16 runs, published halfway build) — condensed

First 10 s: fade-in to a sunset salt flat with a stone arch; below, a "murky brown box" with a chain mirroring it. A weight appears on
the chain, a spire builds on the arch at the mirrored spot, Hooke's quote fades in. Thought: physics toy / museum exhibit; the quote made it
click. Felt: calm, "huh, neat". Wanted to grab the chain.

Understood the rules unaided: drag the chain, the arch mirrors it; release after pulling leaves a weight that becomes a spire; overload or a
hard yank topples the arch; masons rebuild it on timber centering to match the chain. "You design the arch by hanging weights."

Delighted: the quote landing as the spire built; the first collapse (surprise + "oops" guilt); the scaffold rebuild ("time-lapse of masons");
**the cathedral moment** (necklace of stones → spiky Gothic facade): "genuine 'I made that' joy. The best part by far." The wishbone Y.

Concrete problems (weight these):
- Tapping does nothing — first instinct on a phone. Suggests: a tap hangs a weight.
- Grabbing an existing weight doesn't move it; some pulls near the ends register nothing.
- Chain and arch out of sync: a spire fell off while the weight stayed; a new weight ignored for 13+ s.
- Spires don't sit on the arch: needle-point bases, leaning like pencils; clusters overlap and interpenetrate.
- Tall spires cropped at the top of the phone; the camera never zooms out.
- Rebuild glitches: old arch "levitates" off plinths; blocks hover; a block juts sideways from a half-built spire; a fallen spire half off-screen.
- The brown box reads as muddy: blurry diagonal shadow smudge, a stray dark diagonal line across the top ledge, a dark blob bottom-right.
  No visible hooks where the chain meets the ceiling. "What is the brown box? A cellar? A mirror?"
- Speckle noise everywhere; black dotted block edges; hairy weights (fixed in src since).
- Blocks look like a 3D-primitives demo (improved in src since).
- Quote's last word wraps alone on a phone.
- Bored: dead calm when idle (90 s, zero motion); 5–8 s staring at rubble before the scaffold; the collapse–rebuild loop is identical by the 4th time; no goal.

Would send: yes, to an architecture/physics friend, with instructions ("drag, don't tap; hang 6 weights; wait 20 s; you get a cathedral").

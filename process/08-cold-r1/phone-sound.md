# Cold round 1 · phone with sound (21 runs, 8 with audio, published halfway build) — condensed

First 10 s: dusk arch on a salt flat over "a flat brown wall" with a chain hanging like its dark reflection. Felt it was "quiet,
sculptural, a bit eerie". At about 4 s a ball appears and blocks fall onto the shoulder: "oh, it's alive". The quote lands at 9 s and it
clicks: the chain is the arch upside down. Calm and pleased; wanted to grab the chain. No sound until the first touch (browser policy).

Understood unaided: the chain is the blueprint; balls become turrets (heavier means taller); dragging bends the arch live; big changes
collapse it; formwork appears and it is rebuilt along the chain; a V gives a Gothic arch with a spire; tapping a ball removes it.

Delighted: the turret dropping in during the intro; **the arch bending as a live mirror while dragging ("the aha, the best thing")**;
the crash ("satisfying, slightly guilty"); the masons' time-lapse; pulling a V and getting a cathedral ("pride… Gaudí"); "cat ears".

Concrete problems (weight these):
- Tapping does nothing (again the first instinct). The same gesture gives different results: a small tug sometimes adds a ball and
  sometimes nothing; dragging a ball sometimes makes it heavier and sometimes it snaps back.
- Tapping a ball: the turret waits 3–4 s to fall, so cause and effect come apart. Fallen pieces never clear; some lie half off-screen.
- **Dead time after a collapse: 5–10 s of a still pile and silence ("did I break it?"). Their ONE change: rebuild within about 1 s and
  fill the gap with sound, so pull, crash and rebuild feel like one motion.**
- Towers run off the top of the phone screen. Turrets sit on a slanted base touching at one corner and look glued on or hovering.
- The struck scaffold stays inside the arch "as a smaller, off-centre second arch for 10+ s".
- Speckle on the salt flat (black salt and pepper), grainy shadows while moving, jagged dark edges (src has the fixes; verify).
- The heavy ball turns lemon or eye shaped. Blocks look like marshmallows or sugar cubes (charming but toy-like).
- A thin dark line runs across the top band of the wall.
- Phone emulation dipped to 24–29 fps during collapses.
- Sound: the wind is sub-bass (~23 Hz), inaudible on a phone. Collapses jump from silence to crashes peaking at about 1.05 (clipping).
  Rebuild knocks are pleasant (about 5 per second, 1–2 kHz). Turret thud is soft. Taps that do nothing make no sound.
- Idle: frozen after the intro (the sun never moves). By the 5th cycle the loop is identical. The quote is shown once and gone.

Would send: yes, to an architecture/physics/calm-toy friend, with instructions ("drag, not tap… pull it into a V").

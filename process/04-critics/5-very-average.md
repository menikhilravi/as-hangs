# Critic 5 · The "very average at best" verdict (condensed)

Failure codes: REMAKE · THIN VERB · INVISIBLE MODEL · CAPTION · AUTHORED · ONE BEAT · MUTED · WINDOW (the real thing moves people because it
is real) · FAKE PAYOFF (what must look real is the listed fake).

**KEEP (3/36):** LINOTYPE · CANUTE · AS HANGS. Close calls killed: OBSCURA (best first frame; WINDOW + REMAKE), STILL WARM (AUTHORED +
THIN VERB + INVISIBLE MODEL: Newton cooling over ten minutes is indistinguishable from a linear fade), THERMAL.

Top 3: 1 LINOTYPE · 2 CANUTE · 3 AS HANGS.

- LINOTYPE: highest floor of the 36 (brass, oiled steel, a slug cooling from dull red, metal impacts are code's home ground). Trap: an exhibit
  (grey CAD parts on scripted paths, labels, one clack sample). Fix: a *machine, not an animation*: each matrix a physical body falling down
  its own channel with its own travel time, spacebands really wedging, the distributor really sorting by tooth code. Then the real machine's
  behaviour arrives unscripted: type faster than mats fall and letters transpose; no backspace, so a botched line becomes ETAOIN SHRDLU into the
  hellbox; a loose line squirts lead; the distributor is the "how does it know?" moment; slugs remelt. Shoot five macro moments, don't model
  1,500 parts. Spike: a dozen mats clattering into the assembler at macro range must read as photography.
- CANUTE: arc from physics, not prose. Risk: the water (swash) reads as a fluid-sim demo; the creator's habit is to answer "looks like a sim" with
  more rheology. Fix: *the sea is the mortar*: only damp sand holds (capillary bridges), dry slumps to repose, saturated flows, so the best
  building sand is what the last wave wet. One gesture (dig = the dug sand becomes the wall). No score; the setting sun is the clock. Spike:
  one swash cycle over wet sand at golden hour must read as a photograph.
- AS HANGS: purest verb-is-the-idea; small sim, so the work can't hide in systems. Risk: stone that moves (morphing arch = CG surface; path tracer
  noisiest exactly then); a Kangaroo tutorial with a mirror shader. Fix: *stone never morphs*: while you pull, the world above is bare ground or
  timber centering; on release, voussoirs drop into place one by one while the render converges; change the chain under a finished building and
  the new weight arrives before the new shape: the thrust line leaves the masonry, hinges open, it comes down, then rebuilds. Oversold: one chain
  makes an arch, not a cathedral (nets need an editor or a pre-hung model; walking inside adds a second mode). Spike: one limestone arch in low
  raking sun, path-traced to convergence, must read as stone, not plaster or CG.
- Shape of an escaping idea: input is the raw material of a physical process; the physics bites (failure visible); one material code renders
  exactly, provable in one still; stakes and ending from physics. "If none of the spikes reads as a photograph, start from the most
  photographable material and look for the verb inside it."

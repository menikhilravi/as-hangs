# Critic 3 · A stranger on a muted phone, 10 seconds (condensed)

**KEEP (5/36):** AS HANGS · STILL WARM · ARBORGLYPH (marginal) · SCRAPE · MURPHY.
Close kills: TOTALITY, DUNK, FREEFALL. Would bet on two: **STILL WARM, AS HANGS**.

Top 3: 1 STILL WARM · 2 AS HANGS · 3 SCRAPE.

Key points
- STILL WARM: at room temperature a thermal room is nearly flat; the palette range set by a white-hot mug collapses the room into
  one purple, which goes black in glare. Rooms read in thermal only through *authored* small differences (cold window, warm fridge coils,
  cold corners). Fix: open on the moment of leaving: footprints brightening toward a door still swinging shut, a white-hot handle.
  The first swipe becomes "follow them". Undersold: on a phone your warm finger really touches the glass; the print is literal.
- AS HANGS: muted, nothing tells you stone is heavy; a smoothly bending arch reads as a rubber band. Fix: the piece performs its own
  verb once before touch (a sack drops onto the chain off-centre; above, a spire heaves up, settles with dust from its joints, its shadow
  lengthens). Portrait stacks the worlds: thumb works below, result happens above, never under the thumb. The lower half goes dark in glare,
  so the payoff must happen above. A tiny figure at the arch's foot turns a curve into a building.
- SCRAPE: the hook is the satisfying peel (pressure-washing-video pleasure); open with a flake already lifting, a sliver of 70s green under
  it, the pencilled height chart right below, where a thumb lands.
- MURPHY: surest laugh in the set, but past the laugh it's text; butter-down is invisible side-on (needs butter squelching at the edges).
- Phone mechanics nobody mentioned: a pull-down drag triggers pull-to-refresh; long-press opens text selection; edge swipes go back;
  iOS tilt needs a permission prompt; the keyboard covers ~40%.
- Kills by pattern: dark openings (OBSCURA, SLOW LIGHT, THUNDER, THIN ICE, FIRST LIGHT, EVERY SUNSET, STALACPIPE, CRANK; night/deep blue:
  LIGHTS OUT, 52 HZ, KETTLE, PLACE OF HONOR); sound-is-the-core (THUNDER, THIN ICE, 52 HZ, NOON, STALACPIPE, KETTLE, LINOTYPE, THERMAL);
  keyboard (ALEPH, STRATA, MOBILE, LINOTYPE); meaning in captions (HOLD STILL, ETCH, SLOW LIGHT, EVERY SUNSET, 52 HZ, KETTLE, PLACE OF HONOR,
  BRONZE, TANGLE, OVERHANG).
- Survivors share: the first frame shows its own verb; the result appears beside or behind the thumb, not under it.

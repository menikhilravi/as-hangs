# Critic 2 · The jaded creative technologist (condensed)

**KEEP (5/36):** AS HANGS · STILL WARM · THUNDER · LINOTYPE · TANGLE. Closest kill: FREEFALL.
Top 3: 1 TANGLE · 2 LINOTYPE · 3 AS HANGS. Conditional 4th/5th: STILL WARM (forensic version), THUNDER (with fixes).

The principle: most of the 36 are *reveals* (content exists, you uncover it: ETCH, SCRAPE, OBSCURA, LIGHTS OUT, ARBORGLYPH, EVERY SUNSET,
ALEPH, STRATA). A jaded audience has scratched off a thousand. Survivors run on *consequence*: physics computes something from what you
did that you couldn't have predicted, and hands it back in a form you can name and check by eye (a knot type, a slug with your typo
cast in it, a cathedral that stands or falls).

- TANGLE: the only concept that made them laugh that they hadn't seen. Risk: self-intersection makes the named knot a lie. Use XPBD with
  substeps and continuous segment–segment collision (~150–250 segments), validate offline against Raymer & Smith's knotting-probability curve,
  pull the ends tight after each shake so the eye can check the label, name chirality via Jones polynomial. Ceiling raiser: *the phone is the
  box* (DeviceMotion) plus a pocket mode ("11 minutes in your pocket: figure-eight, 4₁"); make it a charging cable. The unknot must be a
  punchline, not a failure (knotting is ~50%, not "every time").
- LINOTYPE: risk is a labelled museum explainer (loses to Ciechanowski's watch). Ceiling raiser: *no backspace* (a mistake means
  "etaoin shrdlu", cast anyway, into the hellbox), the machine's own 90-key keyboard instead of the phone's, and the distributor as act two
  (matrices ride the toothed bar back to their channels by tooth code).
- AS HANGS: risk is "a Kangaroo GIF with path tracing" that is spent after the third spire; wonderlattice #61 could ship first; heavy art bill.
  Ceiling raiser: make the verb two-way. Pull down = load = a spire the arch can carry. *Push up* = the chain drapes over your finger, and the arch
  above takes a shape that could only stand if held from the sky, so it cracks and falls block by block (rigid voussoirs, friction). Let go and
  the fall rewinds. Stone stands only where a chain would hang; no Gaudí model's stone ever falls.
- STILL WARM: undersold. It's a forensic game: every surface is a stopwatch (mug, kettle, bed, shower tiles, a car bonnet outside); order the
  last hour by cooling alone while the evidence evaporates. "In thermal, the simulation is the footage" should be the headline.
- THUNDER: oversold as written (nobody A/Bs squiggles by ear; a screen stroke has no depth). Needs a draggable listener separate from the
  camera to replay the same bolt from elsewhere, segments re-lighting in sound-arrival order (also its only muted mode), close strikes so
  energy stays above phone-speaker range.
- FREEFALL: buried ending: shallow-water blackout on the ascent, the screen going black three metres below a bright surface.
- Factual catches: 52 Hz carries fine in the channel (physics doesn't make the loneliness); NOON's "all arrive together" point generally doesn't
  exist with >3 towers; WOBBLE's theorem fails on steps/craters.

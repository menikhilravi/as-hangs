First 10 s: dim, slightly grainy dusk at 0.3–1 s; "read it straight away as the arch's upside-down twin"; light rises 1.5–4 s, a bag
drops, a spire in three pieces is lowered on a rope onto the mirrored spot; the quote from ~4 s. Calm, "like a museum piece", "oh,
clever". Wanted to tap the arch or grab the chain.

Understood: two worlds mirrored at the ground line; bag = spire at the mirrored point (tapping either world adds the pair);
**"the pink line is the ideal shape, the chain flipped over … if it stays inside the stones the arch stands; if it leaves them the
arch collapses"**; unseen builders rebuild to the new chain shape on centering; each pillar is a straight chain.

Delighted: the opening reveal + quote ("an instant intellectual click"); the wordless tutorial; dragging the crown sideways and
watching the arch peel apart; **the rebuild — "a time-lapse of masons … it forgives you … a nice echo of the theme, since everything is

  edge as you add weight, so a collapse is something you cause and can see coming.**

- Unless it collapses the arch never visibly changes ("the flipped-chain idea looked faked" until one heavy bag).
- Blur/jagged/mush during every interaction (shared machine, but every run); **"speckled patches after collapses: rectangular
  clouds of dots hanging over the salt flat for 20–30 s"**; dotted dark edges along centering posts and seams.
- **Thorny spires on the legs** stick out sideways at odd angles and overlap ("thorns or a cactus"); one at the very foot.
- A bag dragged off vanishes (no fall); one new bag faded in half-transparent; ropes come from nowhere; the centering is plain sticks.
- A side chain pulled straight down didn't move but its pillar fell. Long press glows and does nothing; empty swipes silent.
- Sound: tap thump mostly 100–180 Hz (lost on a phone); wind constant after the first touch with slow gusts; collapse rumble +
  crash; ~10 s of clacks; no music. Idle: nothing changes; same loop after 2–3 minutes.
